# Code of Conduct

NetMask follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We welcome participation regardless of identity, background, ability, or experience. Treat people with respect, discuss technical disagreements constructively, and accept feedback in good faith.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. These standards apply in issues, pull requests, discussions, and when representing the project elsewhere.

Report conduct concerns privately to **Bello Bilal** at `bb2010ng@gmail.com`. Include relevant context and links where possible. Do not post private complaints or identifying information in a public issue.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content and apply proportionate warnings or participation restrictions. Reports are handled with attention to privacy and fairness.

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, by the [Contributor Covenant community](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
